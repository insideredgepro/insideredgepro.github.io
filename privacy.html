Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

InsiderEdgePro is designed to collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account sign-in (optional): if you choose to sign in, authentication is handled by Google OAuth through Supabase, our authentication provider. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google solely to identify your session. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-BM57ZCD9G3) to understand aggregate site usage — pages visited, approximate region, device type. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data according to Google's privacy policy.
  • Local storage: we store your dashboard preferences (filters, selected view, cached data) in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.
  • We never ask for payment details — the service is free.
  • We do not send marketing emails. Your email is used only for authentication.

Data we display

All insider trading data shown on InsiderEdgePro comes from public regulatory filings (SEC EDGAR, STOCK Act disclosure portals, SEDI). Names of corporate insiders and members of Congress appear in those filings as a matter of public law.

Your choices

  • Use the site without an account: previews of every dashboard are available without signing in.
  • Delete your account: sign out and request deletion via Discord or GitHub; we will remove your authentication record from Supabase.
  • Opt out of analytics: use a browser extension such as an ad/tracker blocker, or enable your browser's Do Not Track setting.

Data retention & security

Authentication records are stored by Supabase on secured infrastructure. We retain your account record only while your account is active. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics defaults (14 months).

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
