About InsiderEdgePro

Last updated: November 2026

InsiderEdgePro is a free, independent dashboard that tracks legal insider trading activity across three public data sources: SEC Form 4 filings in the United States, STOCK Act disclosures from the US Congress, and SEDI filings in Canada.

Why we built it

When corporate executives buy their own company's stock with their own money, it is one of the most closely watched signals in markets. The same goes for stock trades disclosed by members of Congress. This data has always been public — but it has been buried in raw government databases that are painful to search, or locked behind paywalled tools charging $20–50 per month.

InsiderEdgePro exists to make that data usable by everyone. No paywall, no subscription — sign in free and see the full feed.

What we track

  • US insider purchases — every open-market buy reported on SEC Form 4, filtered to trades of $25,000+ to surface high-conviction signals.
  • Congressional trades — STOCK Act disclosures from US senators and representatives, including trades made by their spouses.
  • Canadian insider purchases — SEDI filings for TSX and TSX-V listed companies, filtered to CAD$15,000+.

Who runs it

InsiderEdgePro is built and maintained by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with the SEC, SEDI, the US Congress, or any brokerage or financial institution. You can follow the project's data updates and source code on GitHub, or join the community on Discord, where real-time alerts are posted for notable insider buys and congressional trades.

What InsiderEdgePro Does

InsiderEdgePro is a free, independent tracker for public insider trading disclosures. It monitors three official data sources — SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings in the United States, STOCK Act Periodic Transaction Reports from the U.S. Congress, and SEDI insider reports in Canada — and turns them into real-time, searchable feeds with cluster buy detection, per-person portfolios, and performance tracking.

The project was built on a simple observation: insiders and elected officials disclose their trades to regulators, but the raw filings are scattered, hard to search, and impossible to follow in real time. InsiderEdgePro aggregates them in one place, for free, with no paywall. Nothing on this site is financial advice — it is a reorganization of public records, and every trade shown here can be verified against the original government filing.

Is InsiderEdgePro free?

Yes. Every feed, portfolio, and tracker on the site is free. Creating an account unlocks full trade histories and alerts, also free — no credit card is ever required.

Where does the data come from?

Exclusively from public government sources: SEC EDGAR for U.S. corporate insiders, House and Senate disclosure systems for congressional trades, and SEDI for Canadian insiders. No data is estimated, scraped from unofficial sources, or behind paywalls.

Is this financial advice?

No. InsiderEdgePro reorganizes public filings for informational purposes only. Insider purchases are one signal among many, and you should do your own research or consult a licensed advisor before making investment decisions.
